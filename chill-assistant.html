Where Chill is today

This is version 0.3, the voice backend: consent checks, encrypted voiceprints, match scoring with rate limits and lockout, an event-only audit log, and delete endpoints. The mobile app talks to it behind a flag.

The voiceprint model is a placeholder until milestone 4, so this isn't real speaker verification yet. The conversation above is a simulation.
